# Obelisk Club — privacy-first Mac & iOS apps

**[obelisk.club](https://obelisk.club)** is a catalog of 130+ small, focused apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, plus 240+ free browser tools that never upload your files, built by [Peter Zhang](https://github.com/peterzhang0216).

Every app follows the same rules: **runs on-device, needs no account and tracks nothing.** Paid features are mostly a one-time unlock; a few apps (SnapPro, PhotoBridge, ShotKit, NearBeam) also offer an optional subscription next to a lifetime purchase.

## Free browser tools

Everything runs in your browser; files are never uploaded. All of them live at **[obelisk.club/tools](https://obelisk.club/tools)**.
